3. Selective Abstention Protocol Frame
Honest AI Refusal, Low-Confidence Warning Banner, Data Failure Logs & Clarification Chips
FRAME 03 • ABSTENTION
⚠️ The 3 Machine-Enforced Abstention Triggers
  • 1. Sparse History (<30 Days): Refuses baseline analysis for newly launched products or categories.
  • 2. Contradictory Evidence: Stale marketing data (>26h) + cross-source agreement <0.50 triggers pause.
  • 3. Low Confidence (<60%): Overall 5-factor composite score falls below recommendation threshold.
💬 Interactive Clarification Query Chips
  • Zero-Guessing Philosophy: Replaces hallucinated advice with explicit failure diagnostic logs.
  • Interactive Chips: Clickable prompt pills auto-populate the Analyst Chat drawer.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Recovery: Prompts business users to refresh stale pipelines or adjust baselines.

4. Driver Attribution & Diagnostics Frame
OLS Panel Regression, Pearson/Spearman Matrix, Business Rules & IsolationForest Anomalies
FRAME 04 • DRIVERS
🔬 Statistical Attribution & Correlation Check
  • OLS Panel Regression: Displays standardized β strength bars, R² fit, and p-value significance.
  • Dual Correlation Matrix: Evaluates linear Pearson $r$ alongside rank Spearman $ ho$ to flag non-linear drift.
  • Feedback Weight Tuning: Analyst corrections automatically penalize disproven driver weights.
🌲 Anomaly Detection & Breadcrumb Drill-Down
  • IsolationForest Anomalies: Unsupervised ML flags multi-dimensional outlier dates.
  • Auditable Business Rules: Independent IF-THEN triggers (e.g. stockout_rate > 10%).
  • Hierarchical Drill-Down: Interactive navigation: Revenue → Region → Category → Product.
